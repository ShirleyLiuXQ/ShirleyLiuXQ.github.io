I'm a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Statistics at Oxford, working with [Prof. Patrick Rebeschini](https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/~rebeschi/). My research aims to develop <span class="kw">statistical and information-theoretic foundations</span> that make machine learning more efficient and reliable, especially when data is heterogeneous, high-dimensional or changing over time. Towards this goal, I'm currently focusing on <span class="kw">training dynamics</span>, <span class="kw">sequential decision-making</span> and <span class="kw">uncertainty quantification</span>. 

I completed my PhD in <span class="kw">information theory</span> and <span class="kw">high-dimensional statistics</span>  at Cambridge, advised by [Prof. Ramji Venkataramanan](https://rv285.github.io/).  

During an internship at Microsoft Research,   I designed error correction codes for a novel glass-based optical data storage technology. This work  recently appeared in [*Nature*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10042-w).
