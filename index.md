I'm a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Statistics at Oxford, working with [Prof. Patrick Rebeschini](https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/~rebeschi/). My research aims to develop <span class="kw">statistical and information-theoretic foundations</span> that make machine learning more efficient and reliable, especially when data is heterogeneous, high-dimensional or changing over time.


Towards this goal, I'm particularly interested in studying <span class="kw">training dynamics</span>, <span class="kw">sequential decision-making</span> and <span class="kw">uncertainty quantification</span>. I completed my PhD in information theory and high-dimensional statistics  at Cambridge, advised by [Prof. Ramji Venkataramanan](https://rv285.github.io/). 

I was an intern with Microsoft Research Cambridge in 2023, where I designed error correction codes for a novel glass-based optical data storage technology. This work has recently appeared in [*Nature*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10042-w).
